# Part II — Theory of Agency

The **world→mind** half: what may be *pursued* and *done*. Seven volumes, complete as a
system. Part II is not a sequel to [[Part I — Theory of Knowing|Part I]] — it is its
**reflection under direction-of-fit reversal**, joined at the shared **Observation** node.

## The volumes (one line each)
- **Vol I · Ontology** — an agent is a system that **couples both directions of fit in a
  loop** (reads the world to change it toward an end). Agency is a *spectrum*, not a predicate.
- **Vol II · Value** — *reward ≠ value* (the spine; forced by the addict, the obsolete
  drive). Value is the **rootless root**; below the terminal floor everything is entailed.
- **Vol III · Decision** — a decision is the act that closes deliberation into a
  **commitment**. **Commitment ≅ Warrant** (the practical licence). Quality = *warrant*,
  not outcome (`outcome:decision :: truth:belief`). → feeds [[Planner — Vol IV Runtime]].
- **Vol IV · Planning** — `Plan = Commitment ⊕ Dependency ⊕ Irreversibility` (temporal
  order *induced*). **Planning ≅ Inquiry is a homomorphism whose kernel is the
  irreversibility of action** (non-ergodic field). → the [[Planner — Vol IV Runtime]] bridge.
- **Vol V · Execution** — new primitive **Guidance** (steer, not trigger — solves the
  deviant-chain problem, which stays OPEN). Execution is the **payment point** where the
  reversible internal is spent irreversibly against the world → *sources* Vol IV's kernel.
- **Vol VI · Learning** — a frozen-weight model *cannot* learn; **learning relocates to a
  persistent external store that changes future inputs**. → the [[Proprioception — Learning]] bridge.
- **Vol VII · Governance** — the second-order regulation of action; **the Security Theory
  is Vol VII specialized to authority** ([[Capability Escalation & Threat Model]]). The
  capstone. → the [[Governance — GPS-2]] bridge.

## The through-line
Each volume added a primitive and a **No-X → No-Y** theorem; irreversibility of action is
the kernel that keeps separating Acting from Knowing (Vols IV, V, VI). See
[[Recurring Structures]].
