# Recurring Structures

The three patterns that organize the entire [[Theory Stack Map|theory stack]] — evidence
it has one skeleton, not sixteen unrelated docs.

## 1. Direction of fit (the spine)
- **mind→world** (belief): adjust the mind to match the world → *truth*. Additive,
  reversible, non-consuming.
- **world→mind** (value/intention): adjust the world to match the mind → *success*.
  Subtractive, **irreversible, consuming**.
- [[Part I — Theory of Knowing]] is the whole mind→world half; [[Part II — Theory of
  Agency]] is the world→mind half. They meet at **Observation**. The asymmetry — *to know
  is to add, to act is to unmake* — is the **kernel** that keeps Acting from being Knowing
  run backward (surfaces in Planning, Execution, Learning).

## 2. The "No-X → No-Y" family (8 constitutive theorems)
One per node; identical three-part shape (analytic core + deflationary perimeter + open
foundation):

| Node | Theorem |
|---|---|
| Belief/Warrant | No warrant → no knowledge (**C1**) |
| Question | No question → no inquired knowledge (NQ1) |
| Goal | No goal → no agency (T1) |
| Value | No value → no decision (VT1) |
| Commitment | No commitment → no decision-as-settled (DT1) |
| Plan | No dependency → no plan |
| Execution | No guided intervention → no execution |
| Learning | No credit-assignable feedback → no learning (LT1) |
| Governance | No complete mediation → no governance (GT1) |

## 3. The one foundation-hole seen eight ways (the open bottom)
The stack is **determinate in the middle, open at the foundation** — and every open bottom
is the *same hole*: **the point where a system's commitments must enter from outside
itself.**
- ultimate ground (warrant) · first question (inquiry) · origin of ends (goal) ·
  authority of terminal value · bindingness of commitment · the problem of induction
  (learning) · legitimacy of the governor (governance).

> A cognitive-and-agentive system cannot manufacture its own foundation — it cannot
> certify its own warrant, author its own values, ground its own induction, or legitimate
> its own governor. **Knowing exactly where you must receive your foundation from outside
> is the stack's final and most-warranted result** — and building SkynetClaw means
> choosing, deliberately and accountably, what to put in each hole.
