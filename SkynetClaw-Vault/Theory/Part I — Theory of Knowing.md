# Part I — Theory of Knowing

The **mind→world** half: what may be *believed* and what may be *sought*. Nine recovered
theories, complete as a system. Full text in `docs/`.

## The nodes
1. **Belief** — the primitive: a counterfactually-invariant, mind→world disposition.
2. **Observation** — the sensor; the node **shared** with the Acting loop (the bridge).
3. **Causation** — interventionist (Pearl `do`); recovered here, *used* by Acting.
4. **Boundaries** — the limits of the knowable.
5. **Estimation** — belief under uncertainty (the machinery that *transfers* to Decision).
6. **Evaluation → Meta-Evaluation (CEE)** — normativity applied to the knowing process.
7. **Warrant** — the licence to assert-as-known. **C1: no warrant → no knowledge**;
   asserting a zero-warrant belief AS warranted is a *lie about warrant*. → the
   [[CEE — Warrant Runtime]] bridge.
8. **Inquiry** — the forward search: what to ask to reduce the Unknown per cost; a
   *stopping rule* (EIG/VoI vs cost). Blind to unconceived alternatives (its frontier).

## The load-bearing results that reach into the runtime
- **C1** (Warrant) → the CEE overclaim detector — the first bridge.
- **Inquiry's stopping rule** → reused as Decision's closure rule and the Planner's
  round-stopping (theory imported into Acting with no new axiom).
- **Estimation** → transfers directly to [[Part II — Theory of Agency|Decision]] under
  fit-reversal.

## Open at the bottom
The *ultimate ground* of warrant and the *first question* of inquiry are OPEN — two of the
[[Recurring Structures|one hole seen eight ways]].
