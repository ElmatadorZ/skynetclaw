# Internal Contradictions — cross-mission consistency audit

> OBJECTIVE 2: search the four prior missions for contradictions; destroy each or mark unresolved.

## C1 · Disposition (Mission I) vs Cause (Mission III) · RESOLVED
- **Tension:** I leaned dispositional (a belief is a counterfactual pattern, not a hidden pusher);
  III concluded the belief-*state* is a genuine cause. A pattern that is "not an extra pusher"
  seemingly can't be a "cause".
- **Resolution:** under the **interventionist** criterion III actually adopted, a cause *just is*
  an invariance-under-intervention — which is *also* what a disposition is (invariance-under-
  counterfactual-conditions). ∴ disposition and cause are one object on two counterfactual axes
  (BELIEF_THEOREMS T9). No contradiction. **RESOLVED** (SUPPORTED), at the cost of committing to
  interventionist causation (which III did).

## C2 · Content determinate (Mission I) vs Content underdetermined (Mission II) · RESOLVED
- **Tension:** I: belief *has* content (s3). II: behaviour *underdetermines* content (Quine, s7).
- **Resolution:** content *exists* (the invariance is keyed to world-conditions) but is
  *individuated* only up to a holistic equivalence class. Having-content and coarse-individuation
  are compatible. **RESOLVED** (SUPPORTED).

## C3 · "No persistence" (Mission I) vs "Stability signature" (Mission II) · RESOLVED
- **Tension:** I: persistence not necessary. II: SIG-3 requires counterfactual *stability*.
- **Resolution:** stability = reproducibility of the *disposition*; persistence = storage of a
  *token*. A re-derived disposition is stable without a stored token. Different properties.
  **RESOLVED** (SUPPORTED). (This is the load-bearing distinction of the whole programme.)

## C4 · "No essence" (Mission IV) vs "One axiom" (this mission) · RESOLVED
- **Tension:** IV: belief has no classical essence. This mission compresses to a single principle
  — which *looks* like an essence.
- **Resolution:** the axiom is **relational and grain-indexed**, not a shared intrinsic property;
  its "at the grain where invariance holds" clause *generates* graded membership (hard core / soft
  periphery). A *first principle of grade* is not an *essence*. **RESOLVED** (SUPPORTED) — and it
  is the subtlest point in the unification.

## C5 · "Observable only through behaviour" (II) vs "Belief is a real cause" (III) · RESOLVED
- **Tension:** if only behaviour is observable, how is *causal* (not merely correlational) status
  established?
- **Resolution:** II's "behaviour across **counterfactual/manipulated** conditions" already
  includes intervention; invariance-under-intervention is exactly what III needs. The two missions
  use the *same* counterfactual-behaviour instrument. **RESOLVED** (SUPPORTED).

## C6 · "Warranted only if it makes a difference" (III) vs "Natural kind" (IV) · RESOLVED
- **Tension:** a difference-making criterion is pragmatic; natural-kindhood sounds metaphysical.
- **Resolution:** IV *explicitly* adopted III's pragmatic criterion — the kind is retained at the
  core (where it makes a difference) and subdivided at the periphery (where it does not). Aligned
  by construction. **RESOLVED** (SUPPORTED).

## C7 · Direction-of-fit as a sharp joint (I/IV) vs graded boundaries everywhere (IV) · PARTIALLY OPEN
- **Tension:** IV leaned on direction-of-fit as the robust cut, yet also showed all cuts are graded
  (besires blur it).
- **Resolution:** direction-of-fit is the *strongest* cut but not exceptionless; it is downgraded
  to **LIKELY** (not SUPPORTED-exceptionless). The theory absorbs this by making T4 LIKELY. No
  contradiction, but a **weakened** joint. **RESOLVED as a downgrade**, not a clean win.

## The one genuinely UNRESOLVED item · OPEN / UNKNOWN
### C8 · Content vs vehicle efficacy
- Mission III left it **UNKNOWN**; the axiom quantifies over the belief-*variable* and cannot say
  whether its **content** or its **vehicle** carries the invariance.
- This is **not a contradiction** between missions (all four are silent or agree it's open) — it is
  a **shared boundary of the evidence.** It is the *only* place where the surviving results do not
  fix a single answer, and therefore the *only* opening for two incompatible theories to persist
  (REDTEAM_OF_UNIFICATION). **OPEN / UNKNOWN.**
