# Boundary Matrix — where belief ends against each neighbour

> Continues BELIEF_TAXONOMY / BELIEF_OBSERVABILITY / BELIEF_CAUSAL_THEORIES. For each
> neighbouring construct: what it **shares** with belief, its **unique** mark, the **operational
> test** that separates it, a **failure case** where the test blurs, and **confidence**. Science
> first (philosophy of mind, epistemology, cognitive science). Tags: SUPPORTED / LIKELY /
> SPECULATIVE / UNKNOWN. No software, no AI evaluation.

## Two axes that organize the matrix (SUPPORTED)
1. **Direction of fit** (Anscombe; Searle): belief = mind-to-world (revise to match world);
   desire/goal/preference = world-to-mind (act to make world match). *The cognitive/conative cut.*
2. **Occurrent vs standing / graded vs flat-out / attitude vs content**: separates belief from
   its own products (prediction), its degrees (credence), and its vehicles (representation).

| **Knowledge** | content, holding-true, guides action | + **truth** + **justification/safety** (world-side) | not internally/behaviourally separable — add veridicality & anti-luck (Gettier) | over-learned true justified belief ≈ knowledge | SUPPORTED |

| **Habit** | disposes action | **outcome-insensitive** (S–R) | outcome-devaluation: habit persists post-devaluation; belief-guided action drops | habitual expression of a live belief | SUPPORTED |
| **Preference** | dispositional | **comparative ordering** (conative) | direction of fit (world-to-mind); revealed by *choice between* options | instrumental preference tracking a belief | SUPPORTED |
| **Desire** | pro-attitude, action-relevant | **world-to-mind** direction of fit | mismatch test: desire drives world-change; belief yields to world | wishful believing (desire distorting belief) | SUPPORTED |
| **Goal** | directs action | desire + **represented target + commitment** | persistence-through-obstacles + means-ends re-planning | a believed *ought* that motivates | LIKELY |
| **Identity** | doxastic self-attributions | **motivated / asymmetric updating** | disconfirmation asymmetry (resists counter-evidence) | any strongly-held belief resists updating | LIKELY |
| **Model / world model** | generative, predictive | **the space** (dynamics), not a point | change one belief (hidden-state estimate) with the model fixed | Bayesian-brain: beliefs = model parameters | LIKELY |

| **Intuition** | dispositional, action-guiding | **fast, non-inferential** (System 1) | reflective-override: intuition persists against endorsed belief (illusions) | a reflectively-endorsed intuition | SUPPORTED |
| **Trust** | involves expectation | **three-place, agent-directed, normative** (A trusts B to φ; betrayal ≠ mere error) | vulnerability/normative test: violated trust warrants *betrayal*, not just updating (Baier) | reliance grounded purely in a factual belief about reliability | SUPPORTED |

| **Rule** | can be believed | **normative prescription** (ought) / a followed regularity | grasping-a-rule vs acting-in-accord (Wittgenstein/Kripke rule-following) | believing that one ought to follow R | LIKELY |

## What the matrix already reveals (SUPPORTED)
- **No single test separates belief from *all* neighbours** — it takes *several*: direction-of-fit
  (vs conative), evidence-update (vs memory/habit), attitude-vs-content (vs representation/
  meaning), graded-vs-flat (vs credence), occurrent-vs-standing (vs prediction), endorsement (vs
  expectation/hypothesis/assumption).
- **Every row has a failure case** where the boundary blurs — no neighbour is cleanly walled off.
  This foreshadows the family-resemblance question (FAMILY_RESEMBLANCE / REDTEAM_OF_BOUNDARIES).
- **The most robust separator is direction-of-fit + evidence-sensitivity** — it survives across
  the most rows and is the operational spine of "belief, not something else."
