# Ontology of Belief — the concepts shared across surviving definitions

> The smallest set of *concepts* (not implementations) that every surviving model of belief
> presupposes. Recovered by asking: what must exist for *any* of T1–T11 (BELIEF_TAXONOMY) to
> be true? Tags: SUPPORTED / LIKELY / SPECULATIVE / UNKNOWN.

## The five shared entities
Every surviving account, however it disagrees on carrier and individuation, presupposes these:

### O1 · A CONTENT (the *about-what*) · SUPPORTED
A state of affairs the belief concerns, with **satisfaction conditions** (it can be
right or wrong / accurate or not). Without content there is nothing to believe. Present in
all of T1–T11 (even implicit/procedural beliefs are *about* something to be belief at all).

### O2 · A BEARER (the *who/what*) · SUPPORTED
A system to which the belief is ascribed — the organism/agent whose dispositions the belief
organizes. (Interpretationism (T4) makes the bearer + interpreter a pair; the bearer is still
required.)

### O3 · A CARRIER (the *how-realized*) · SUPPORTED (that one exists) / UNKNOWN (which kind)
*Some* physical/computational realizer of the disposition. The models **disagree on its
type** — stored token (T1/T6), disposition-profile (T2), model-parameter (T8), sufficient
statistic of history (T9), attributed pattern (T4). What is shared: **there is a carrier**;
what is *not* shared: **that the carrier is the belief-object itself, or that it stores the
belief between uses.** → The carrier is ontologically necessary; its *persistence-as-storage*
is not (it may be a *generator* that re-derives the belief).

### O4 · A DISPOSITION (the *what-it-does*) · SUPPORTED
A counterfactual profile: *were the content-relevant situation to arise, the bearer would
act/predict/bet as-if the content.* This is the one entity every operational account makes
load-bearing (de Finetti's betting, Dennett's prediction, functional role, POMDP action,
predictive-processing action). **The disposition is the belief's functional essence.**

### O5 · A DYNAMICS (the *how-it-could-change*) · LIKELY
A space of possible successor states under evidence — the belief's *revisability envelope*.
Some models make change active (Bayes/AGM/PP: O5 is an operator); others make it merely
possible (dispositionalism: O5 is a modal fact). Shared minimum: a belief is the *kind of
thing located in a space of alternatives it could move to.* (A state with an empty successor
space is an axiom/reflex, not an empirical belief.)

## The ontology, minimally
```
        O2 BEARER
          │  ascribed to
          ▼
   ┌─────────────────┐   is about        ┌────────────┐
   │   BELIEF         │──────────────────▶│ O1 CONTENT  │ (satisfaction conditions)
   └─────────────────┘                    └────────────┘
     │ realized-by            │ constituted-by         │ situated-in
     ▼                        ▼                        ▼
  O3 CARRIER            O4 DISPOSITION          O5 DYNAMICS
  (stored OR            (counterfactual         (revisability
   re-derived —          act/predict as-if)      envelope)
   type UNKNOWN)         ← the essence
```

## What is NOT in the ontology (SUPPORTED)
- **Storage/persistence-of-the-belief-object** — belongs to O3's *type*, which is variable;
  not a shared entity. (This is the mission's target assumption; it is **not ontologically
  fundamental**.)
- **A discrete boundary per belief** — individuation is *not* shared: holism (T-web) and
  interpretationism deny sharp per-belief boundaries; representationalism asserts them. →
  "a belief" as a countable object is **model-relative, not ontological.** LIKELY.
- **Language / propositional form** — only T1/T6 require it; tacit, procedural, POMDP beliefs
  have no sentential form. Not shared.
- **Consciousness / phenomenology** — no operational model requires it. Not shared.

## The ontological core (LIKELY)
> **Belief = the pairing of a CONTENT with a counterfactually-stable DISPOSITION of a BEARER,
> realized by some CARRIER and situated in a DYNAMICS of possible revision.** The carrier's
> *kind* — stored or reconstructed — is a free variable of the ontology, not a defining term.
