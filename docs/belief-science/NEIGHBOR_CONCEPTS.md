# Neighbour Concepts — operational comparison + where experts disagree

> Prose companion to BOUNDARY_MATRIX: the operational separators in depth, and (OBJECTIVE 3) the
> cases where experts **systematically** disagree — recovered as live disputes, not resolved.
> Tags: SUPPORTED (well-attested position/debate) / LIKELY / SPECULATIVE / UNKNOWN.

## Clusters (belief's neighbourhood has structure)
- **Cognitive/doxastic** (mind-to-world): knowledge, memory, prediction, expectation, credence,
  confidence, hypothesis, assumption, world-model, representation, understanding, intuition.
- **Conative** (world-to-mind): desire, preference, goal.
- **Procedural**: policy, habit, skill, rule.
- **Relational/normative**: trust (agent-directed), identity (self-directed).
- **Semantic substrate**: meaning, representation (the *content/vehicle*, not an attitude).
The single sharpest cut is **direction of fit** (cognitive vs conative); the next is
**attitude vs content** (belief vs meaning/representation).

## Systematic expert disagreements (OBJECTIVE 3) · SUPPORTED that these are unresolved
### belief vs knowledge · SUPPORTED
JTB (justified true belief) → **Gettier (1963)** cases show JTB insufficient → decades of failed
repairs. **Knowledge-first** (Williamson): knowledge is *unanalysable and prior*; belief is the
weaker, sometimes "botched", derivative — reversing the traditional order. Dispute: is knowledge
belief-plus, or belief a degenerate knowledge? **Unresolved.**

### belief vs credence · SUPPORTED / UNKNOWN (relation)
Are flat-out belief and graded credence **one kind or two**? **Lockean thesis**: belief = credence
above a threshold — but the **lottery paradox** (rational high credence each ticket loses, yet
irrational to *believe* it loses) and **preface paradox** strain the threshold view. Positions:
reduce belief to credence / reduce credence to belief / **dualism** (both fundamental). No
consensus.

### belief vs expectation · SUPPORTED (disciplinary split)
**Predictive processing** (Friston, Clark, Hohwy) *identifies* belief with probabilistic
expectation (posterior over hidden states) — dissolving the distinction. Traditional epistemology
keeps them apart: expectation can be automatic/sub-personal; belief is a personal-level attitude
one can endorse or disavow. The split is **disciplinary**, not merely verbal.

### belief vs understanding · LIKELY / UNKNOWN
Is **understanding** a species of knowledge/belief, or a **distinct epistemic good** (Kvanvig,
Pritchard, Grimm)? Disputes: is understanding **factive**? is it **ability** (grasping
dependencies, answering "what-if") rather than propositional? Understanding seems **holistic and
graded** where belief is discrete and binary. **Unresolved.**

### belief vs representation / model · SUPPORTED
**Representationalists** (Fodor) posit discrete belief-representations; **connectionists/
dynamicists** deny stored discrete representations; **Bayesian-brain** reframes beliefs as
*parameters of one model* rather than a set of propositional attitudes. Whether "a belief" is a
discrete thing at all is contested.

### belief vs memory · LIKELY
Are stored **semantic-memory facts** beliefs, or a source of belief? Is memory a *doxastic*
faculty? The overlap (a remembered fact one also believes) makes clean separation contested at
the semantic-memory boundary.

## The operational separators, restated (SUPPORTED)
| To separate belief from… | apply the test |
|---|---|
| desire/preference/goal | **direction of fit** (mismatch → who yields?) |
| memory | **evidence-update to the present** + provenance |
| habit | **outcome-devaluation** |
| policy/skill | **novel-state generalization / know-how vs assent** |
| credence | **odds elicitation** (graded vs flat-out) |
| prediction | **standing generator vs token output** |
| expectation | **personal-level endorsement** |
| hypothesis/assumption | **act-as-true vs bracketed-for-purpose** |
| representation/meaning | **the doxastic attitude toward a content** |
| trust | **normative vulnerability** (betrayal vs error) |
| understanding | **grasp of a system's dependencies (counterfactual span)** |

## Recovered lesson (SUPPORTED)
Belief is not separated from its neighbours by *one* essence-test but by a **battery of
orthogonal cuts**. Where the disputes are sharpest (knowledge, credence, understanding), the
disagreement is **not empirical noise but a structural non-convergence** across traditions — a
sign that the concept's *boundary*, not just its measurement, is at issue (FAMILY_RESEMBLANCE).
