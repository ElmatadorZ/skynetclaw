# Red-Team of Unification — destroy the single-axiom theory

> OBJECTIVE 4/6: attack Axiom A. Try to (a) show a clause is unnecessary, (b) replace the theory
> with fewer assumptions, (c) prove one coherent theory cannot hold. Uses only prior evidence.
> Tags: SUPPORTED / LIKELY / SPECULATIVE / UNKNOWN / FALSIFIED / RETRACTED.

> **A** = *belief = a counterfactually-invariant relation between world-tracking states and
> actions, existing at the grain where the invariance is exploitable for prediction/intervention.*

## Part 1 — is any clause removable? (attack each; keep only load-bearing)
| Drop this clause | Re-admits (a falsified/idle position) | Removable? |
|---|---|---|
| *counterfactually-* | mere actual behaviour / correlation — FALSIFIED as belief (Mission II Blockhead; III firewall) | **No** |
| *-invariant* | one-off states / momentary guesses — fail stability (Mission I N3) | **No** |
| *world-tracking (input arm)* | contentless output-only states = habit/reflex — FALSIFIED as belief (BOUNDARY T-DEVAL) | **No** |
| *…to actions (output arm)* | epiphenomenal inner states — FALSIFIED (Mission III) | **No** |
| *exploitable for prediction/intervention* | idle-wheel beliefs — violate parsimony/warrant (Mission III T10) | **No** |
| *at the grain* | a sharp classical essence — FALSIFIED (Mission IV) | **No** |
Every clause blocks a *distinct* already-destroyed position ⇒ **no clause is unnecessary.**
Compression to fewer clauses **fails**. (SUPPORTED)

## Part 2 — can a *different* smaller theory replace it?
- **"Belief = behaviour"** (0 counterfactual apparatus): FALSIFIED — underdetermination + masking
  (Mission II). Smaller but wrong.
- **"Belief = correlation between state and action"**: FALSIFIED — correlation breaks under
  intervention; belief must be *invariant under intervention* (Mission III CS-1). Smaller but wrong.
- **"Belief = stored representation with intrinsic content"** (representationalism): **not smaller**
  (adds a stored token + intrinsic semantics) and partly FALSIFIED (persistence not necessary,
  Mission I). A *rival*, not a *reduction*.
- **"There are no beliefs"** (eliminativism, 0 axioms): FALSIFIED — the belief-state is an
  interventional difference-maker (Mission III). Cannot go to zero.
∴ no coherent theory with **fewer** commitments fits the surviving evidence. (SUPPORTED)

## Part 3 — the one attack that survives: content vs vehicle
- **Attack:** A quantifies over the belief-*variable* but does not say whether its **content** or
  its **vehicle** carries the invariance (Mission III UNKNOWN). So A is *incomplete*: two
  incompatible completions exist — (i) content-efficacious belief, (ii) vehicle-efficacious belief
  with content riding along.
- **Does it destroy A?** No — A is **true on both completions** (both agree the *state* is the
  invariant difference-maker). A is *silent*, not *false*, here.
- **But it blocks full unification:** on this single axis the surviving evidence does not select
  one theory. ∴ **exactly one** two-way fork remains — the content/vehicle question — and it is a
  *gap in the evidence*, not a *contradiction in the theory*. **UNKNOWN, survives.**

## Part 4 — attack the deflationary commitment
- **Attack:** A is deflationary/functional-interventionist; a representationalist would reject it,
  so "one theory" only holds *relative to* deflationary commitments the missions chose.
- **Verdict:** **LANDS as a scope-limit, not a refutation.** A is the minimal theory that fits the
  *surviving* evidence; the representational surplus it omits was FALSIFIED (persistence) or left
  UNKNOWN (content/vehicle, phenomenal). So A is not *the only conceivable* theory — it is *the
  smallest theory consistent with what survived*. A committed representationalist keeps a *larger*
  theory, not a competing *minimal* one. The unification is **conditional on the deflationary/
  interventionist stance the four missions already adopted** — stated honestly, **LIKELY**, not
  SUPPORTED-unconditionally.

## PRIMARY QUESTION — one coherent theory, or must multiple incompatible remain?
**One coherent theory suffices for every finding the four missions *settled*** — a single axiom
(A) derives all SUPPORTED/LIKELY theorems, and the seven cross-mission tensions all resolve
(INTERNAL_CONTRADICTIONS). **Multiple incompatible theories are forced only on the single unsettled
axis — content vs vehicle** — where the evidence is silent. ∴ **near-total unification: one theory,
one open fork.** (SUPPORTED for the unification; UNKNOWN for the fork.)

## FINAL QUESTION — the irreducible scientific theory of belief

> **Belief is a counterfactually-invariant relation between a system's world-tracking states and
> its actions — real at the grain where that invariance is exploitable for prediction and
> intervention, carried by any substrate, individuated only as coarsely as the invariance allows,
> and posited only where it makes a difference.**

- **One axiom.** Every surviving result is a theorem of it (BELIEF_THEOREMS).
- **Nothing less:** each clause blocks a distinct falsified alternative; removal collapses the
  theory (Part 1). Zero axioms is falsified (the state is a difference-maker).
- **Nothing more:** persistence, essence, discreteness, avowal, phenomenal surplus were all
  eliminated; adding them re-admits refuted positions.
- **Its single frontier** — the one thing it cannot, on present evidence, decide — is whether the
  invariance is carried by the belief's **content** or its **vehicle**. That question *is* the edge
  of the science of belief: everything inside it is one theory; the edge itself is **UNKNOWN.**
