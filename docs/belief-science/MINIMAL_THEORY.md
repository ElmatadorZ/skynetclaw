# Minimal Theory — the shortest coherent theory of belief that survives

> The single axiom, its scope, and what it does and does not claim — stated as compactly as the
> surviving evidence allows. Nothing added beyond OBJECTIVES 1–5. Tags: SUPPORTED / LIKELY /
> UNKNOWN.

## The theory (one axiom)
> **Belief is a counterfactually-invariant relation between a system's world-tracking states and
> its actions — from the world-conditions it covaries with, to the actions it disposes — existing
> exactly where, and at the grain at which, that invariance is exploitable for prediction and
> intervention.**

That is the whole theory. Everything below is exposition, not addition.

## What each word does (no clause is idle — proven in REDTEAM_OF_UNIFICATION)
- **counterfactually-invariant** — makes it a *disposition* and a *cause* at once, and forces
  *counterfactual* (not merely actual) observation. Drop it → mere correlation/behaviour (Mission
  II/III firewall). **Load-bearing.**
- **relation between world-tracking states and actions** — supplies *content* (input keying) and
  *direction of fit* (world→state→action). Drop it → contentless reflex. **Load-bearing.**
- **exists exactly where … exploitable for prediction and intervention** — supplies *warrant*
  (difference-making) and blocks over-posit. Drop it → idle-wheel beliefs (parsimony violation).
  **Load-bearing.**
- **at the grain** — supplies *coarse individuation* and the *cluster* structure (hard core / soft
  edge). Drop it → false sharp essence (Mission IV FALSIFIED). **Load-bearing.**
- **any substrate (implicit in "relation")** — supplies *carrier-independence* (storage or
  re-derivation). Drop it → the falsified "belief = persisted object." **Load-bearing.**

## Scope — what the theory covers (SUPPORTED)
Generates, as theorems (BELIEF_THEOREMS), every surviving claim of all four missions: dispositional
nature, stability, content, direction of fit, revisability (restricted), behavioural
observability, coarse individuation, causal (interventional) status, difference-making warrant,
cluster-kindhood, and the externality of the belief/knowledge line.

## Scope — what the theory deliberately does NOT decide
- **Content vs vehicle** (UNKNOWN): which property of the state carries the invariance. The theory
  quantifies over the belief-*variable*; this is left open by the evidence, not by oversight.
- **Knowledge** (out of scope by design): truth + anti-luck justification are world-side; the
  theory is about the *internal invariant relation* only. Belief ⊂ the theory; knowledge ⊄.
- **Phenomenal "what-it-is-like"** (out of scope): no surviving result required it.

## Theory type (SUPPORTED)
- **Deflationary / functional-interventionist.** It identifies belief with a role/relation, not an
  intrinsic representational essence. This is not a free choice: the non-deflationary surplus
  (representational essence beyond dispositions; phenomenal belief) was FALSIFIED or left UNKNOWN
  by the prior missions, so the *minimal* theory that fits the *surviving* evidence is deflationary.
- **A first principle of grade, not an essence** — so it coexists with Mission IV's "no classical
  essence" (INTERNAL_CONTRADICTIONS C4).

## Minimality claim (LIKELY)
No shorter theory fits the surviving evidence: each clause blocks a *specific* falsified
alternative (correlation, contentlessness, over-posit, sharp essence, object-persistence). Removing
any clause re-admits a position the prior missions already destroyed. Compression to **one axiom**
is achieved; compression to **zero** (eliminate belief) is blocked by the SUPPORTED result that the
belief-state is an interventional difference-maker. ∴ **one axiom is the floor.**
