# Belief — Differential Diagnosis

> Operational separators that distinguish *belief* from the states it is routinely confused
> with. Each row gives the **discriminating test** (an intervention that dissociates them),
> recovered from the literature. Tags: SUPPORTED / LIKELY / SPECULATIVE / UNKNOWN.

## The master separators (two do most of the work)
- **Direction of fit** (Anscombe; Searle) · SUPPORTED — on a **belief↔world mismatch**, a
  *belief* changes to fit the world (mind-to-world); a *desire/goal* drives action to change
  the world to fit it (world-to-mind). One perturbation dissociates belief from desire.
- **Outcome-devaluation / contingency-degradation** (Dickinson; Adams) · SUPPORTED — devalue
  the outcome (or degrade action→outcome contingency): *goal-directed, belief-guided* action
  drops immediately; *habit* persists. One manipulation dissociates belief-guided action from
  habit.

## Full differential table
| Confused with | What it is | Discriminating test (dissociation) | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Memory** | a record *of the past* (provenance-bearing) | Update-on-evidence: a *belief* revises toward current evidence; a *memory* retains its past content even when disbelieved (one can remember a claim one rejects). Also: belief has no essential pastness. | SUPPORTED |
| **Knowledge** | belief that is *also* true + justified (JTB, Gettier-caveated) | **Not behaviorally separable from the inside** — truth & justification are *world-side* facts. Two systems with identical belief-behaviour can differ in knowledge only by external truth/causation. → knowledge adds unobservable conditions. | SUPPORTED |
| **Habit** | stimulus→response, outcome-insensitive | **Outcome devaluation** (above): habit persists post-devaluation; belief-guided action does not. | SUPPORTED |
| **Policy** | state→action mapping (procedural) | **Novel-state generalization**: a belief (state-estimate + model) transfers to unseen states via the model; a cached policy fails off-distribution. Model-based vs model-free dissociation. | LIKELY |
| **Goal / desire** | a target state to bring about | **Direction of fit** (above): on mismatch, belief yields, desire persists-and-drives-action. | SUPPORTED |
| **Emotion** | valenced/arousal state with action tendencies | **Belief–affect dissociation**: a phobic "believes" (endorses) the object is safe yet fear persists; affect can run counter to belief → separable. Emotion lacks satisfaction-conditions of the belief's content. | SUPPORTED |
| **Identity** | self-model / self-attributions | **Asymmetric updating**: identity-beliefs resist disconfirmation (motivated reasoning); ordinary beliefs update more symmetrically. Identity = a *subset* of beliefs with special dynamics, not a different kind. | LIKELY |
| **Intuition** | fast, non-inferential disposition (System 1) | **Reflective override / endorsement**: intuitions can *conflict* with reflectively-endorsed belief and persist (cognitive illusions: the Müller-Lyer lines still *look* unequal after you believe they're equal). Dissociable by reflective endorsement vs automatic pull. | SUPPORTED |
| **World model** | the *generative structure* of possibilities | **Whole vs part**: the world-model is the *space*; a belief is a *particular setting/posterior* within it (predictive-processing framing). Test: change one belief (a hidden-state estimate) while the model (dynamics) stays fixed. | LIKELY |

## Two clarifying consequences (SUPPORTED)
1. **"Knowledge" is not black-box-observable.** Every behavioural probe reaches at most
   *belief*; truth and justification are external. So a purely behavioural science can certify
   *belief*, never *knowledge*. (This bounds every later protocol.)
2. **Belief sits between memory and action, defined by two counterfactual signatures** —
   *direction of fit* (vs desire) and *outcome sensitivity* (vs habit) — plus *evidence-update*
   (vs memory) and *generalization* (vs policy). These four dissociations are the operational
   skeleton of "belief, not something else."

## Residual ambiguity · UNKNOWN
Where a state is *simultaneously* memory-derived, habitual, and belief-like (over-learned true
beliefs), the dissociations can co-fire and the categories blur. The literature treats these as
*mixed* states, not counterexamples — but clean separation is **UNKNOWN** in such cases.
