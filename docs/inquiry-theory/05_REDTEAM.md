# 05 — Red Team

> Pure epistemology. Deliverable 10. Attack the Theory of Inquiry; keep only what
> survives. Each attack: threat · verdict (SURVIVES / DOWNGRADED / OPEN) · residue.
> Then the net position, and the stack-closing statement.

---

## Attack 1 — Where does the FIRST question come from? (Meno strikes back)
I4 says a question is the recognition-shape of an answer — but forming the *right*
question needs prior knowledge, which needed prior questions… regress. **Verdict:
OPEN (inherited).** Question-formation bottoms out in something non-inquired: innate
question-readiness, or pre-rational generators — *surprise, prediction-error,
curiosity, anomaly* — that produce a first question without one preceding it. The
theory does not derive the origin; it inherits inquiry's own foundational regress,
the exact dual of Warrant's regress. **Residue:** the *bottom* of inquiry (the first
question) is OPEN, as the *bottom* of warrant (the ultimate ground) was. The stack is
honestly open at both ends.

## Attack 2 — EIG is blind to unconceived alternatives (the deepest hit)
Expected information gain is computed against a prior over a *fixed possibility space*.
You cannot ask about — or value a question about — a possibility your space omits
(zero prior mass ⇒ zero EIG). So EIG-optimal questioning is **blind to the unknown
unknowns** — the "problem of unconceived alternatives" (Stanford). **Verdict:
DOWNGRADE (serious).** The information-theoretic core (file 01) is trustworthy only
*within* a possibility space it cannot itself certify complete. The theory measures
inquiry *inside a frame*; it cannot value the question that would *enlarge the frame*.
**Residue:** file 01's metric is SUPPORTED as *intra-frame* quality, silent on
frame-expansion — and frame-expansion is where the greatest discoveries live.

## Attack 3 — EIG undervalues revolutionary questions (Kuhn)
The best scientific questions often have *low* immediate EIG (speculative, low
discriminating power under the current prior) yet are transformative because they
*reframe* the space. **Verdict: DOWNGRADE.** EIG-as-quality holds for *normal-science*
questions (discriminating within a paradigm); it systematically undervalues
paradigm-shifting questions — precisely the Discovery class (file 00, Q3) the mission
prized. **Residue:** the quality metric works for normal inquiry, poorly for
revolutionary inquiry. Honestly, the theory measures best exactly what matters least,
and worst what matters most — the frontier.

## Attack 4 — The Stopping Rule assumes computable VoI/cost
VoI needs foreseeing how an answer would change the decision — often not possible
before asking; EIG needs the prior; cost needs foreseeing the observation. **Verdict:
DOWNGRADE.** The Stopping Theorem is *well-defined* but not always *computable* — its
inputs are only estimable, inheriting every ground-truth and measurability limit of
the estimation theory. **Residue:** the stop is a normative *ideal* (the right target),
approximated in practice, not a decidable procedure.

## Attack 5 — Is inquiry really question-first? (serendipity / abduction)
Much discovery is accidental: a *noticed anomaly* generates the question, so
observation can precede the question (Peirce's abduction; penicillin). **Verdict:
PARTIAL HIT — refines.** The strict linear "question → observation → evidence" is an
idealization; the real structure is the *cycle* of file 02, where surprise/prediction-
error enters at the observation node and *evokes* the first question (reconnecting to
Attack 1). **Residue:** ordering is a policy over a graph, not a line; anomaly is a
legitimate entry point, not a violation. The linear picture is downgraded to one mode
of a cyclic process.

## Attack 6 — Is NQ1 a tautology?
"Inquired knowledge answers a question" may be true *by definition of "inquired"* — not
substantive. **Verdict: PARTIAL.** NQ1 *is* near-analytic (hence SUPPORTED-but-shallow);
its substantive, falsifiable content is the **corollary**: *reach is bounded above by
question-forming capacity.* **Residue:** credit the corollary, not the near-tautology.
The deep claim is that an inquirer's ceiling is its questions, and *that* is refutable
(exhibit discovery exceeding what its questions could frame).

## Attack 7 — Self-application: what question is this document the answer to?
The theory must survive being turned on itself. This document answers a *design/
discovery* question ("how do questions form, when stop?"). Gates: presupposition
(questions have recoverable structure — satisfiable, the literature exists) ✓;
partition (the rival erotetic theories) ✓; recognition (falsifiers stated) ✓. **Verdict:
SURVIVES at LIKELY warrant** — testimonial + inferential, defeasible, self-consistently
claiming no more. A theory of inquiry that could not be posed as a well-formed question
would refute itself; this one can.

## Net position after the red team

| Plank | Post-attack |
|---|---|
| Question = partition + presupposition + aim (I1–I3) | **SURVIVES** (SUPPORTED) |
| Meno resolved via recognition-shape (I4) | SURVIVES (SUPPORTED) |
| EIG = value of a question | SURVIVES **only intra-frame**; blind to unconceived alternatives (Attack 2) |
| EIG = question *quality* | DOWNGRADED — fails for revolutionary/frame-expanding questions (Attack 3) |
| Stopping Theorem | SURVIVES as a **normative ideal**, not always computable (Attack 4) |
| Expanding frontier (Theorem E) | SURVIVES (LIKELY) |
| Dead vs dormant | SURVIVES (SUPPORTED) |
| Question-first ordering | DOWNGRADED — one mode of a cycle; anomaly can seed (Attack 5) |
| No-question → no inquired knowledge (NQ1) | near-analytic; the substantive part is the **ceiling corollary** (Attack 6) |
| Origin of the first question | **OPEN** (inherited regress, Attack 1) |

**What the Theory of Inquiry IS, after being attacked:** a theory of *intra-frame*
inquiry — how, given a possibility space, to ask the question that most reduces the
Unknown per cost, and when to stop. It is strong there. It is *weak exactly at the
frontier*: it cannot value the question that enlarges the space, cannot certify the
space complete, and cannot say where the first question comes from. Its highest-value
object — the frame-breaking question — is the one it measures worst. Stated, not hidden.

## The stack-closing statement

The two directional theories bracket knowledge and both bottom out in an open
foundation:

- **Warrant (backward):** the licence to *assert-as-known* is bounded above by the
  warrant possessed. Its foundation (the ultimate ground) is OPEN.
- **Inquiry (forward):** the reach of *discovery* is bounded above by the questions
  formable — and by the possibility-space conceivable, whose completeness is unknowable
  from inside. Its foundation (the first question) is OPEN.

Together: **knowledge is fenced from both ends — what may be claimed, and what may be
sought — and the fence-posts at both extremes rest on ground the theory cannot see.**
This is not a gap to be patched; it is the mature result. A theory of cognitive systems
that knew its own foundation completely would, by Attack 2, be claiming a possibility-
space it cannot certify — the very overreach the whole lineage exists to forbid. The
stack is complete in its middle and *honestly, necessarily* open at both ends. That
openness — knowing precisely where knowing stops — is the last thing the theory
delivers, and the one it is most warranted in asserting.
