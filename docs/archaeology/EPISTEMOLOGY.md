# The Epistemology of Genesis Mind (recovered)

> What the organism treats as knowledge, evidence, uncertainty, ignorance, failure, proof,
> and the un-knowable — recovered from the epistemic organs' own words and the Trust docs.
> Tags: SUPPORTED / LIKELY / SPECULATIVE / UNKNOWN.

## What counts as KNOWLEDGE? · SUPPORTED
A conclusion that is **repeated, consistent, and cross-corroborated** — never a single
observation. Evidence:
- lesson-synthesis: *"experience is not learning. A log is not a lesson ... only when
  REPEATED evidence in a SIMILAR context produces a CONSISTENT [conclusion]."*
- first-principles: promoted only at *"support ≥ 5 AND confidence ≥ 0.70 AND ≥ 2
  independent systems (cross-validation)."*
- theory: a principle becomes a **theory** only when it *"holds across MANY domains ...
  corroborated by Causal hypotheses in ≥ 2 domains."*
→ Knowledge = **corroborated, calibrated, cross-domain regularity**, and even then labelled
a *candidate* until a human/later protocol promotes it.

## What counts as EVIDENCE? · SUPPORTED
Outcome data tied to action: successful-vs-failed episodes (causal), lift-over-baseline
(reinforcement/attribution), predicted-vs-observed confidence (calibration), and — at the
write boundary — a **tool call that sourced the datum** (shadow_gate LIVE-DATA gate requires
a live tool before live-data may be written). A claim with no outcome and no source is **not
evidence**.

## What counts as UNCERTAINTY? · SUPPORTED
A first-class, *measured* quantity, not a vibe. calibration surfaces *over/under-confidence*;
decision emits *"reasoning, risks and uncertainties"*; research-agenda ranks topics by
*"how unsure we are (uncertainty)."* Uncertainty is something the organism **quantifies and
prioritises around**.

## What counts as IGNORANCE? · SUPPORTED
Mapped explicitly via the Rumsfeld matrix (unknowns.py): **known-knowns / known-unknowns /
unknown-unknowns.** Ignorance is not absence-of-data — it is a *tracked epistemic state*
("surfaced gaps, open questions, contradicted beliefs"). curiosity is the *"Gap Discovery
Protocol."* → the organism keeps a **ledger of what it does not know.**

## What counts as FAILURE? · SUPPORTED
(a) Cognitive: a promoted belief **contradicted by recent evidence** (belief-revision drift)
or a prediction the outcomes **did not match** (calibration miss). (b) Engineering: a red
regression/chaos test, or an unproven completion. Failure is **an observation to record**,
not an error to suppress — Epic Trust: *"every failure becomes a permanent regression test."*

## What counts as PROOF? · LIKELY (there is no absolute proof; only graduated support)
The organism has **no notion of final proof** — only escalating support tiers: candidate →
cross-validated principle → cross-domain theory → (Kuhn) stable paradigm. Even a paradigm is
provisional (paradigm.py tracks *"PARADIGM SHIFT in progress"* when anomalies accumulate).
The closest thing to "proof of done" is completion-evidence: *"PROVE completion ... against
REAL evidence the run already produced"* — i.e. proof = **checkable evidence the criteria
hold**, never assertion. → Proof is *provisional, evidential, and revisable*.

## What can NEVER become truth? · SUPPORTED
- A claim resting on a **single source / single mission / single detector** — barred by the
  *"≥ 2 independent systems"* and *"no single mission can jump a weight"* rules.
- **Unsourced live data** the model merely generated — barred at the write boundary
  (shadow_gate).
- A **mere assertion** ("TASK_COMPLETE", "it works") with no supporting evidence — barred by
  completion-evidence.
- **Correlation mistaken for cause** without a control-vs-test experiment (experiment.py's
  entire reason to exist).
- (Constitution / Trust) anything requiring an **irreversible act without a human gate** —
  cannot be acted-as-true autonomously.

## The one-sentence epistemology (LIKELY)
> **Knowledge is corroborated, calibrated, cross-domain regularity — always provisional,
> always tagged with its uncertainty, never sourced from one place, never asserted without
> evidence, and (almost always) promoted only under human judgment.**

This is a **fallibilist, corroboration-based, human-supervised empiricism** — and it is the
lineal descendant of Genesis V1's original gene ("flag claims needing evidence; humans
adjudicate"), now grown organs but keeping the human at the point of promotion.
