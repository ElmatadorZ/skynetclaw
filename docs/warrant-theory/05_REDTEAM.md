## Attack 1 — The regress (Agrippa / Münchhausen). *Fatal to A1?*
A1 says warrant needs a ground; the ground is a belief that needs warrant; → an
infinite regress, or circularity (coherentism), or a dogmatic stop (foundationalism)
— the trilemma no one has dissolved. **Verdict: OPEN (inherited).** The theory does
not resolve the oldest problem in epistemology; A1 *generates* it. The theory must
*pick a horn* (foundational basics / coherence / infinitism), each with a known
cost, and it does not. **Residue:** A1 stays SUPPORTED as a local requirement but
the *global* foundation of warrant is UNRESOLVED and must be declared so. The theory
is a theory of warrant's *structure*, not of its *ultimate ground*.

## Attack 2 — Self-undermining. *What warrants the Theory of Warrant?*
The theory's own claims have warrant only via testimony (the literature) + inference
— defeasible, non-foundational, exactly the lossiest kinds (file 03). **Verdict:
SURVIVES, at reduced warrant.** This is *consistent*: the theory applies its own
T-min and defeasibility to itself and claims only modest, revisable warrant. A theory
of warrant that claimed indefeasible warrant for itself would refute itself; this one
doesn't. **Residue:** the whole document is LIKELY-grade, self-consistently.

## Attack 3 — Externalism eliminates the normative core (W1).
If warrant just *is* production by a reliable process, the "licence/permission" talk
is eliminable — W1 (normativity) is a gloss on a natural fact. **Verdict: DOWNGRADE.**
W1 → **LIKELY-CONTESTED**: on hard externalism the normative reading is optional. The
theory cannot assert warrant is *irreducibly* normative; it can only say the
normative *vocabulary* is indispensable in practice, which reliabilists dispute. A
real hit on the ontology's first plank.

## Attack 4 — The value problem (Meno). *Is warrant idle?*
Why is warranted true belief better than merely true belief? If no added value, the
theory decorates. **Verdict: SURVIVES.** Warrant's value = it makes truth **non-
accidental, repeatable, transferable, and defeasibly trackable** — a lucky true
belief does none of these; a warranted one supports re-use of the method, transfer
to others, and revision under defeat. Warrant is the *counterfactually robust* part
of getting it right (the same counterfactual-invariance that individuates belief in
the belief-science recovery). **Residue:** warrant earns its keep — LIKELY.

## Attack 5 — Is "warrant" even ONE kind? *Category error in the title.*
T-no-analysis (no sufficient conditions) + the source taxonomy (kinds with different
decay/transfer/composition laws) suggest "warrant" is a family-resemblance term, not
a unified kind — so "*The* Theory of Warrant" oversells. **Verdict: DOWNGRADE (the
strongest attack).** Recovered reply: warrant is a **homeostatic cluster kind** (as
belief was) — a common core (truth-connection + defeat-freedom + the lattice) held
together by overlapping conditions, not a classical essence, plus type-specific laws.
**Residue:** the deliverable is honestly *a theory of a cluster + its kind-relative
laws*, not of an essence. The title is a convenience; the content is a family.

## Attack 6 — Is the theory itself falsifiable, or self-protecting?
The "tag CONTESTED and move on" move could immunize the theory against refutation.
**Verdict: PARTIAL HIT.** The genuinely falsifiable content is the SUPPORTED rows
{T-nec, T-insuf, U, T-susp, C1} — each with a real falsifier. The CONTESTED rows are
**not claims but a map of open terrain**; they make no falsifiable commitment, so
they cannot be *credited* as results either. **Residue:** the theory's *asserted*
content is small and falsifiable; the rest is honest cartography. Stated plainly so
the map is not mistaken for territory.

## Attack 7 — Pragmatic encroachment vs A2 (truth-aim).
Warrant seems to depend on *stakes* (high stakes demand more warrant for the same
evidence) → warrant is not purely truth-aimed. **Verdict: DOWNGRADE.** A2 →
**LIKELY**: warrant is truth-aimed *and* possibly stakes-modulated. The purity of
the truth-aim is contested. The collapse results (C1) are unaffected (they concern
truth-connection, which stakes don't remove), but A2's *purity* is dented.

## Attack 8 — Degreed warrant without a metric (from the estimation lineage).
A3 treats warrant as degreed, yet there are no units. **Verdict: SURVIVES.** Warrant
is **ordinal, not cardinal** — a *ranking* (Spohn) or the lattice, coherent without a
number. This is consistent with T-no-analysis (no clean scalar) and with the belief-
science result that graded epistemic states are warrant-tags first, numbers only
where a real frequency backs them. **Residue:** degreed-but-ordinal — LIKELY.

## The headline question, answered after the red team
> *What is the licence that lets a system believe something?*

The recovered answer, stripped to what survives:

**A belief is warranted — licensed as a candidate for knowledge — to the exact
degree that it stands in a defeat-free, truth-connecting relation to a ground, at a
grade no higher than that ground supports, on the total evidence.** The licence is
not truth (a warranted belief can be false), not confidence (a feeling), and not
consensus. It is *the tracked, defeasible, transfer-lossy connection between a
doxastic state and reality that makes getting it right non-accidental.*

And the one result that binds the whole lineage, load-bearing and falsifiable:
**for any system that aims at truth, presenting a zero-warrant belief as warranted
does not merely fall short of knowledge — by C1 it *cannot* be knowledge, so the
presentation is a lie about warrant.** The licence to *assert as known* is bounded,
above, by the warrant actually possessed. That bound — not any feature of any model
or platform — is what every prior mission in this lineage was circling: the point
at which a belief does, and does not, *deserve to exist as knowledge.*
