## Red Team

### Attack 1 — This is smuggled ethics
"Ontology of value, not ethics" is a fig leaf: any theory of value imports value-claims.
**Verdict: SURVIVES.** The volume recovers only the *form* of value (V1–V4: it is a
grounded ranking, representations are conditional) and is *provably* content-neutral —
the paperclip-maximizer is admitted as a *formally valid* value structure (Vol II·02),
and orthogonality (VT4) is stated as a *result*, not evaded. A theory that let "paperclips
is a valid terminal value" through is manifestly not doing ethics. **Residue:** the
volume stops exactly at the is/ought fence and says so; the content of the floor is
declared out of scope (open problem 5), not silently supplied. Honouring the fence *is*
the proof it is not ethics.

### Attack 2 — reward≠value is an assumption, not a finding
The whole spine rests on distinguishing reward from value; but that could be a modelling
choice, and a pure hedonist/RL-reductionist denies it (value *just is* discounted
reward). **Verdict: SURVIVES, and it is the volume's strongest plank.** The distinction
is not stipulated — it is *forced by phenomena*: the addict's "I don't want to want
this" (a reward with a disendorsing value), the evolved drive judged obsolete
(value ≠ its own origin), and the very *statability* of reward hacking and wireheading
(Vol I F3/F4 are *incoherent* if reward = value). A reductionist must call all of these
illusions; the cost of that is denying the manifest first-person and the entire
AI-safety vocabulary. **Residue:** reward≠value is SUPPORTED by inference-to-the-best-
explanation of real cases, downgradable only by paying an implausibly high redescription
cost. The reductionist position is *coherent* but *expensive*, and the volume names the
price.

### Attack 3 — "No Value → No Decision" is a tautology
If "decision" is *defined* as "value-guided selection," VT1 is analytic and empty (the
NQ1 / T1 objection, third time). **Verdict: PARTIAL (as before).** The headline is
near-analytic; the *substantive* content is the corollary chain: **VT3 (everything below
the floor is entailed) + VT1 ⇒ a decider's quality is bounded by its value's quality,
and the value is not produced by the decision machinery.** That is falsifiable and
consequential (VF4: flawless machinery amplifies a wrong floor; the AI-safety punchline).
**Residue:** credit the corollary — *the decision is only as good as the value it
maximizes* — not the near-tautological headline. Same treatment as C1/NQ1/T1; the
*recurrence* of this exact objection-and-answer across four theorems is itself a
datapoint for VT2.

### Attack 4 — The value graph over-rationalizes; real value is messy
The clean `terminal → instrumental → goal → preference → utility` cascade (file 01) fits
an idealized rational agent; real values are inconsistent, context-dependent, framed,
and plural (Kahneman/Tversky framing; constructed preferences). **Verdict: DOWNGRADED
(scope limit).** The graph is a model of *coherent* value and degrades where value is
constructed-on-the-fly or framing-dependent — the same "strong in the middle, weak at
the frontier" shape as Vol I's ontology (deliberative vs enactive) and inquiry's EIG.
The volume already concedes the sharpest form (Q8 incommensurability, VF5) and flags
value *dynamics* as unaddressed (open problem 6). **Residue:** the ontology is a model of
*structured, reflective* value; constructed/framed value is the acknowledged frontier
where it thins. Owned, not hidden.

### Attack 5 — Value-first begs the question against Hume
Choosing "value-first" over "preference-first" (Q4) presupposes a standard above
preference — exactly what the Humean denies. **Verdict: OPEN (honest).** The volume does
*not* claim to refute preference-first; it argues value-first is *not eliminable*
(second-order preferences and akrasia keep re-introducing a standard), and it *proceeds*
value-first because the whole failure taxonomy (reward hacking, wireheading, akrasia)
requires a standard trackers can fail against. **Residue:** value-first is a *working
commitment justified by explanatory power*, not a proof; the Humean alternative is live
and the choice is flagged as CONTESTED, never as settled. The volume buys the standard on
credit and shows what it purchases.

### Attack 6 — Self-application: what is this document's value?
Turn the theory on itself: this document is an *act* (Vol I·05 Attack 6) with a *value*.
Its terminal value = a faithful, minimal, content-neutral ontology of value; instrumental
values = the six files (derived from that terminal value + the "recover-don't-invent"
causal model); its reward-sensor = the red team + the falsifiers (fallible, hence the
grading); and reward≠value was *honoured in the writing* — strong claims graded, content
withheld, the floor's authority left open rather than asserted. **Verdict: SURVIVES.** A
theory of value whose own construction optimized a proxy (impressiveness) over its
terminal value (fidelity) would be a lived VF1; the grading discipline is the defense,
and it is the same C1 the whole stack runs on. LIKELY.

### Attack 7 — Orthogonality makes the volume useless for alignment
If form ⇏ content (VT4) and content is out of scope, the volume says nothing about the
one thing that matters — *which* values. **Verdict: SURVIVES, reframed.** The volume's
*use* is precisely to prove alignment is **not** a knowing-problem: no amount of Part-I
progress supplies the terminal floor (Hume + VT4), *all* openness localizes to one node
(VT3), and the only structural defense against wireheading is holding value distinct from
reward (VT5). Locating the problem exactly — one node, outside the machinery, unbridgeable
from "is" — is *more* useful than a false content-claim would be. **Residue:** the volume
is the *map* that shows where value-content must be *put in*, and proves it cannot be
*derived*. That is the honest service; pretending to derive content would be the
overclaim C1 forbids.

## The symmetry, engaged (not merely accepted)

The proposal to insert this volume rests on a symmetry claim. It survives scrutiny — and
sharpens the earlier hinge-doc table, which had mapped `Belief↔Policy` (conflating the
*primitive* with the *licensed output*). The correct four-row mirror is:

| Role | Knowing | Acting |
|---|---|---|
| **primitive (atom)** | Belief | **Value** |
| **licence** | Warrant | Justification |
| **licensed output** | Knowledge (= warranted belief) | Policy (= justified disposition) |
| **forward search** | Inquiry | Planning |
| **shared sensor** | Observation | Observation |

**Belief ↔ Value** is the load-bearing correction: both are the *ground primitive* their
stack is built from — the thing licence operates on, ontology-first, before any dynamics.
This is the *structural* reason Volume II had to precede Decision: **you recover the
primitive before the dynamics** (Part I did Belief before Warrant before Inquiry;
Part II must do Value before Decision before Planning). The mirror is not decoration — it
*predicted the roadmap gap*. LIKELY, and it retro-corrects the hinge doc's one loose row.
*(Note for later: the hinge doc's symmetry table should be updated to Belief↔Value /
Knowledge↔Policy; recorded here, not silently changed there.)*

## D10 · Summary — what Volume II delivers

**The claim, minimally:** value is the *ordering-ground of pursuit* (world→mind) — the
standard, prior to any goal, that desire, choice, and reward all *attempt to track and
can diverge from*. It is a **grounded ranking** (V1–V2): a partial order with at least
one terminal floor. Below the floor everything is *entailed* (instrumental value, derived
goals, utility) given a causal model; *at* the floor everything is *open* (content and
authority). Utility and reward are **conditional representations** — utility exact only
under VNM, reward a fallible sensor — and conflating either with value is the root of
reward hacking and wireheading.

**The load-bearing results:**
1. **reward ≠ value** — the spine, forced by real phenomena (the addict, the obsolete
   drive), and the sole structural barrier to wireheading. Reward is the value-axis's
   *Observation*: a fallible M→W sensor, disciplined by C1.
2. **VT3 · determinacy below the floor** — the entire value structure is computed from
   the terminal floor + a world model; the *only* open degree of freedom is the floor
   itself. Alignment is localized to one node.
3. **VT4 · orthogonality** — form ⇏ content; competence never supplies the floor; the
   is/ought fence has teeth. This is why value needed its own volume: Part I cannot
   reach across it.
4. **VT1 · No Value → No Decision** — true for discriminating value; the **fourth**
   member of the stack's No-X→No-Y family (C1, NQ1, T1, VT1), same three-part shape,
   same open bottom.
5. **The value graph composes with the agency graph** — Vol I saw failure *symptoms*;
   opening the value node localizes their *root causes* (VF→F), the concrete payoff of
   inserting this volume.

**How it sits in the stack.** Vol I named Value the rootless root and stopped. Vol II
opens it and proves the root is the *only* thing left open — everything else in value is
entailment (VT3). It supplies Decision (Vol III) with a complete primitive, so Decision
can be `Given Value → Decision`, clean. And it lands the fourth No-X→No-Y theorem,
showing the four open foundations of the whole stack (ultimate ground / first question /
first goal / terminal value's authority) are **one hole seen four ways**: *the point
where a system's commitments must enter from outside the system.*

**What Volume II does NOT do (scope, stated so it is not overclaimed):** it gives the
*ontology* of value, not its *content* (which values — ethics, out of scope by design),
not its *dynamics* (how values change — a later concern), and not the *decision procedure*
that uses it (Vol III). It is the noun; the verbs come next. It hands Decision a defined
primitive and a marked boundary — exactly what Vol I handed nothing of, which is why the
insertion was right.

**One-line result:** *No Value → No Decision* — จริง, for discriminating value; and the
reason it is true is VT4 — the value that a decision maximizes can never be manufactured
by the deciding, nor derived from any amount of knowing. The machine is only ever as good
as the end put into it from outside.
