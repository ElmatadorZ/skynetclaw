## The theorems

### LT1 · The Learning Theorem — *No credit-assignable feedback → No learning* (Q10)
**Statement.** A system with no feedback it can *assign to a disposition-component* does not
learn — it at most stores (memory) or drifts (aimless change). Feedback alone is insufficient;
**credit** is the differentia.

**Support: SUPPORTED for occurrence; the strong "correctly assigned" form is a separate norm.**
- *Constitutive reading:* remove credit assignment and an outcome cannot target *what to
  change* → no directed disposition-change → no learning. Follows from LA3.
- *Deflationary perimeter:* superstition *learns* with *wrong* credit — so "credit-assignable"
  (there is a target) is necessary, "correctly assigned" is a norm on quality, not a condition
  of learning. (Same shape as every prior volume: the constitutive core + a quality norm.)

**Verdict on "จริงไหม": Yes** — non-trivially, because it locates learning's hard node
(credit) and explains its signature failure (superstition, LF1). The headline "No feedback →
No learning" is the near-analytic version; the substantive claim is **"No *credit* → no
*directed* learning."** **Falsifier:** genuine learning with feedback but provably no credit
assignment of any kind (blind change that still counts).

### LT3 · The Credit-Asymmetry Theorem (the volume's principal result)
**Statement.** Acting-side learning is **strictly harder** than knowing-side learning, by
exactly the irreversibility of action: to learn about a world you only *observe*, you can
re-observe and check credit; to learn about a world you *changed*, the counterfactual (the
road not taken) is destroyed by the taking, so credit is **inferable only, never checkable**.
`Learning ≅ Belief-revision` is therefore a **homomorphism whose kernel is non-ergodicity** —
the same kernel as Vol IV (`Planning ≅ Inquiry`) and Vol V (the world-boundary asymmetry).
**Support: SUPPORTED.** **Consequences (LIKELY):** (i) real agents *must* explore (deliberately
vary action to recover hidden counterfactuals, LF6); (ii) a *model* (Part I) is the only
partial escape (credit counterfactuals by prediction without re-acting), never total because
the model is itself learned over the spent world. **Falsifier:** acting-side credit shown as
checkable as knowing-side without a model or re-acting.

### LT4 · The Frozen-Seat Theorem (the runtime consequence)
**Statement.** Learning requires a mutable disposition-seat (LA1). If the model's weights are
frozen, the seat relocates to a **persistent external store that changes future inputs**;
by the ontology this store-change *is* learning (cross-episode, outcome-driven, retained).
**Support: SUPPORTED** (LA1 + the frozen-weight case, Vol VI·02). **Consequence:** SkynetClaw's
*learning organ is a proprioceptive memory* — the system's own operational outcomes (failures,
warrant violations, gate blocks, evaluations) mined into a self-model that changes future
context. Today the system has the *memory* (logs) but not the *learning* (the log does not
change decisions) — it sits at the "lookup table" verdict (Vol VI·02) and the bridge moves it
to "Bayesian update." **Falsifier:** the frozen model shown to learn with no external store.

### LT5 · The Return-Confluence Theorem
**Statement.** Learning is the **return confluence** of the figure-eight: it couples
outcome-*observation* (mind→world) to disposition-*revision* (of both belief and policy),
mirroring Decision (the forward confluence). With Learning theorized, all four cross-points of
the two loops now have theories — Observation (input), Decision (forward output), Guidance
(in-act), Learning (return). **Support: LIKELY.** **Consequence:** the stack's loop is
structurally *closed* at the level of theory even where the *system* is still open at the level
of build. **Falsifier:** a genuine learning that revises a disposition with no outcome-
observation input.

## Q9 · Open problems
1. **The problem of induction (the open foundation).** No finite outcomes justify a general
   disposition; LA5 (uniformity) is unestablishable without circularity (Hume). The deepest
   open node — the learning-layer image of warrant's ground / value's authority / the first
   question. OPEN.
2. **Credit assignment in the non-ergodic field.** The counterfactual problem (LT3) — you
   never see both potential outcomes. Mitigated by exploration + models, never dissolved. OPEN.
3. **Stability vs plasticity.** How much to retain vs overwrite (LF4) has no general optimum
   without knowing the world's drift rate — which is itself learned. OPEN.
4. **Value/reward learning without wireheading.** Learning *what* to value from outcome, while
   the outcome-signal is corruptible (LF2, Vol II) — the alignment problem at the learning
   layer. OPEN.
5. **Generalization's limit.** What licenses extending a learned disposition to the unseen
   (VC-bounds inside a frame; open-world beyond it — LF3, the Inquiry frontier). OPEN.
6. **Self-model fidelity.** When the learner learns about *itself* (proprioception), the
   disposition and the thing-learned-about are the same system → the update can change what is
   being measured (reflexivity). Whether a self-model can be stably accurate is UNKNOWN.

## Settled vs open (honest ledger)
- **Settled (SUPPORTED):** the necessary core LA1–LA4; learning ≠ memory ≠ adaptation; credit
  as the differentia (LT1); the credit-asymmetry / non-ergodic homomorphism (LT3); the
  frozen-seat consequence (LT4); node-validation by owned failures.
- **Settled (LIKELY):** the sixth No-X→No-Y (LT2); the return-confluence (LT5); model as
  partial escape.
- **Open:** induction (1, the foundation); non-ergodic credit (2); stability/plasticity (3);
  value-learning without wireheading (4); generalization's limit (5); self-model reflexivity (6).

The pattern holds an eighth time — **determinate in the middle, open at the foundation** — and
the return arc's foundation-hole is **induction**: a learner, like a knower and an agent,
cannot manufacture the guarantee that its past licenses its future. But Vol VI's distinctive
contribution is not another open bottom — it is **LT4**: it tells the system *where its own
learning must live* when its brain cannot learn, turning the theory directly into the next
build (proprioception).
