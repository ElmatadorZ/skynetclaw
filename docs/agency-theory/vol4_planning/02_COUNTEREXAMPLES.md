# Agency — Volume IV · 02 — Counterexamples & Boundary Cases

> Pure philosophy. Objective 5. Ten cases, each ruled **Plan** or **Not-Plan** with the
> discriminating reason, tested against the recovered primitive `Plan = Commitment ⊕
> Dependency ⊕ Irreversibility` (Temporal = induced). The cases are also the evidence
> base that validated the primitive in file 01.

---

## The ten verdicts

| Case | Verdict | Discriminating reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopping list** | **Not-Plan** | no dependency, no order — an unordered *goal-set / checklist* |
| **Recipe** | **Plan** (a *type/schema*) | has dependency + irreversibility + resource; lacks only a committed *token* now |
| **GPS route** | **Plan** | dependency (reach A to reach B) + irreversibility (a passed exit is passed) + committed path |
| **Chess opening** | **Not-Plan** (a *policy*) | contingent state→action map, not a committed particular path |
| **Military campaign** | **Plan** (paradigm) | all four primitives + milestones + revision — the full case |
| **Jazz improvisation** | **Not-Plan** (a *framed reactive* act) | no pre-committed sequence; only a committed *frame* |
| **Reflex** | **Not-Plan** | no commitment, no deliberation, no dependency — a sub-agency reaction |
| **Animal migration** | **Not-Plan** (a *teleonomic policy*) | evolved, unrepresented, un-revised — installed, not held |
| **Construction blueprint** | **Not-Plan** (a *goal specification*) | specifies the *end-state*, not the *means* to build it |
| **Calendar** | **Not-Plan** (a *schedule*) | bare temporal order, no dependency — the sharpest boundary |

## Dissected — the cases that carry weight

### Shopping list & Calendar — *Dependency is necessary* (and Temporal is not sufficient)
A shopping list has *subgoals* (items) but no dependency and no order — buy them in any
sequence. A calendar has *temporal order* but no dependency — the 3pm item does not
*require* the 2pm item's effect. **Neither is a plan.** Together they prove two things:
Dependency is necessary (both lack it, both fail), and **bare Temporal order is not
sufficient** (the calendar *has* it and is still not a plan). This is the counterexample
that most directly *demotes* Temporal from primitive to induced (file 01, Correction 1).
SUPPORTED.

### Recipe — *Plan-type vs plan-token; and irreversibility is present*
A recipe is a *plan* — it has ordered dependent steps (mix before bake), irreversibility
(you cannot un-crack the egg), and consumed resources (ingredients). But it is a
**type/schema**: reusable, unattached to a particular dinner now. A *plan-token* is a
recipe *committed to here, tonight, with these eggs*. This mirrors the Procedure/Plan
boundary (Objective 4) and — importantly — shows the **Irreversibility primitive is
visible even in the most domestic plan**: cooking is non-ergodic (a burnt sauce is not
recoverable), which is why a recipe is a plan and a shopping list is not. SUPPORTED.

### GPS route & Military campaign — *the paradigm, and the revision loop*
The GPS route is a clean plan: a committed path with dependency (arrive at each node to
proceed) and irreversibility (a missed turn forecloses that branch, forcing *replanning*).
The military campaign is the full paradigm — dependency, resources, milestones, phases,
and *essential revision* ("no plan survives contact with the enemy," Moltke/Clausewitz).
The campaign shows the revision loop (file 01 edges e–f) is not optional decoration for
serious plans in a *reactive, non-ergodic* world — which is exactly where planning is most
inquiry-like (adaptive, feedback-gated). SUPPORTED.

### Chess opening & Animal migration — *Plan vs Policy*
Both look like plans but are **policies**. A chess opening is a *contingent* map ("if
Black plays c5, then…") — it does not commit to a *particular* sequence, because the
environment (the opponent) co-determines the path. Animal migration is a *teleonomic
policy* (Vol I): an evolved, unrepresented state→action disposition, not a route the
animal *holds* and *reasons* about. **A plan is a committed particular route; a policy is a
standing contingency map.** (Vol III already separated Decision from Policy; Vol IV
separates Plan from Policy the same way — a plan is temporally-extended committed decision,
a policy is a standing disposition.) The migration case also re-imports Vol I's
teleonomy/agency boundary: unrepresented goal-direction is sub-planning, as it was
sub-agency. SUPPORTED.

### Jazz improvisation — *the framed-reactive boundary (a direct hit on "structured action ⇒ plan")*
Improvisation produces *structured, goal-directed, temporally-ordered* action with **no
pre-committed sequence**. If structured action entailed a plan, improvisation would refute
the ontology. It does not: the improviser commits to a **frame** (key, form, 12-bar
changes) — a thin plan — *within which* the notes are generated *reactively*, in real
time. **The frame is planned; the content is not.** This isolates the exact boundary: a
plan commits to a *structure of means*, not necessarily to every act filling it. It also
previews the file-05 red team: improvisation is the *reactive* stratum of action (the twin
of *perception* on the knowing side), sitting beneath the deliberative planning stratum —
not a counterexample to Planning ≅ Inquiry but a demonstration that *both* have a reactive
floor. SUPPORTED.

### Reflex — *no commitment, no plan*
A reflex is stimulus→response with no deliberation, no commitment, no dependency reasoning.
Not a plan — it is the pure reactive floor (Vol I's minimal/sub-agency). It marks the
lower bound: below commitment, there is behaviour but no plan. SUPPORTED.

### Construction blueprint — *Plan vs Goal-specification (the sharp one)*
A blueprint is *not* a plan — it is a **specification of the goal-state** (what the
finished building *is*), containing no dependency-ordered, resource-bound *means* to erect
it. The *construction plan/schedule* (with its critical path, trades sequencing, and
material logistics) is the plan; the blueprint is its **goal**. This cleanly separates
**Plan from Goal** (Objective 4) using a case people intuitively miscategorize, and it
reinforces that a plan is *means-structure*, not *end-description*. SUPPORTED.

## What the counterexamples establish
1. **Dependency is necessary; Temporal is not sufficient** — shopping list & calendar
   (the demotion evidence). SUPPORTED.
2. **Irreversibility is pervasive in genuine plans** — recipe, GPS, campaign are all
   non-ergodic; the free-field look-alikes are the *non*-plans (checklist, calendar). This
   is the counterexample-side confirmation of file 01's added primitive. SUPPORTED.
3. **Plan is distinct from Policy, Goal-spec, and Schedule** — chess opening / migration
   (policy), blueprint (goal), calendar (schedule). Three of the ten "not-plans" fail by
   being a *different well-defined thing*, which is stronger than failing vaguely.
   SUPPORTED.
4. **Both action and knowing have a reactive floor** — reflex, improvisation, migration
   are unplanned structured behaviour, the twins of perception/instinct on the knowing
   side. This *sets up* the red team rather than threatening the symmetry. SUPPORTED.

## Falsifiers
Any single verdict is refuted by showing the case has (or lacks) the discriminator claimed
— e.g. exhibit a shopping list with genuine dependency structure (then it *is* a plan, and
the reason stands), or a calendar that is a plan despite no dependency (would refute the
demotion). The reactive-floor reading of improvisation is refuted if improvisation is shown
to *be* real-time planning (then it supports, not bounds, the ontology — either way the
ontology survives, which is the point of stating it).
