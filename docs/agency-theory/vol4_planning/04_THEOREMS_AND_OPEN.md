## Objective 10 · The candidate formulas, adjudicated

The mission proposed testing:
- (i) `No Temporal Structure → No Plan`
- (ii) `Plan = Commitment + Temporal Structure`
- (iii) `Plan = Commitment + Temporal Structure + Dependency`
— or proving them wrong. The recovery (files 01–03) adjudicates each.

### PT1 · `No Temporal Structure → No Plan` — **TRUE but for the wrong reason** (refined)
A plan does lack *nothing* if it has no ordering only when it also has no dependency and no
resource conflict — i.e. a genuinely order-free "plan" is a shopping list (Not-Plan). So
`No Temporal → No Plan` is **extensionally true**. But it is true *because* no-temporal
implies no-dependency-and-no-resource (temporal order is their shadow, file 01), **not**
because Temporal is itself the missing primitive. The correct theorem is:

> **PT1′ · No Dependency (and no Resource conflict) → No Temporal Structure → No Plan.**
> Temporal absence is a *symptom*; dependency/resource absence is the *cause*.

Verdict: (i) SUPPORTED as a material implication, but **the implied claim that Temporal is
the operative primitive is FALSIFIED** (file 01 Correction 1, file 03 PF4). Falsifier: a
plan with dependency and resource structure but *no* temporal order (would sever the
shadow from its body).

### PT2 · `Plan = Commitment + Temporal Structure` — **FALSIFIED**
Missing Dependency entirely. A calendar is `Commitment + Temporal Structure` (I commit to
be at these times) and is **not a plan** — it is a schedule (Objective 4, file 02).
Verdict: **FALSIFIED** by the calendar counterexample. Falsifier to overturn: show the
calendar *is* a plan (no one recovers it as one).

### PT3 · `Plan = Commitment + Temporal Structure + Dependency` (the mission's main formula) — **FALSIFIED as sufficient**
Two independent defects (files 01, 03):
1. **Insufficient** — omits **Irreversibility/Resource**, which owns failure modes (resource
   collapse PF3, corner-painting PF8) the three terms cannot produce. A committed,
   dependency-ordered structure over a *free, replayable* field is a *search/derivation*
   (inquiry), not a *plan of action*.
2. **Mis-structured** — lists **Temporal** as a primitive when it is the *induced* order of
   Dependency + Resource (no owned failure, PF4 reduces).

Verdict: **FALSIFIED as stated.** Corrected to PT4. Falsifier to overturn *this* verdict:
reduce PF3/PF8 to dependency/commitment failures (would restore sufficiency), or exhibit a
paradigm plan over a provably non-consuming field.

### PT4 · The recovered minimal primitive — **SUPPORTED**
> **Plan = Commitment ⊕ Dependency ⊕ Irreversibility(Resource)**, with **Temporal
> Structure = the induced partial order** of Dependency + Resource.

Support: SUPPORTED — each term is necessary (file 01, Obj 2), each owns an irreducible
failure mode (file 03), and no counterexample survived (file 02). Completeness for
*creative / open-world* planning is **UNKNOWN** (open problems). This is the volume's
positive result: the mission's three terms become **Commitment (kept), Dependency (kept),
Irreversibility (added), Temporal (demoted to derived)**.

### PT5 · The Elaboration Theorem — *a plan is an elaborated commitment*
**Statement.** A plan is a single Commitment (Vol III) unfolded into a dependency-and-
resource-structured means toward its goal; Planning is the activity of that unfolding and
its revision. **Support: SUPPORTED.** **Consequence:** the Goal→Commitment→Plan spine (file
01) is the identity of a plan, and it is the *precise mirror* of Warrant→Inquiry: **Planning
extends Commitment as Inquiry extends Warrant.** This is the symmetry's true statement — and
file 05 shows it holds at this (form) level while *breaking* at the field-physics level.
**Falsifier:** a plan that is not the elaboration of any commitment (a plan committed to
nothing — a plan-schema, which is a type, not a held plan).

### PT6 · The Non-Ergodicity Theorem — *where Knowing and Acting part*
**Statement.** Planning's field is **non-ergodic**: it contains absorbing/foreclosing states
(resource exhaustion, irreversible missed windows) from which the goal is permanently
unreachable, because action **consumes and unmakes** (world→mind). Inquiry's field is
(idealizing) **ergodic**: the truth remains approachable from anywhere because observation
**adds and does not consume** (mind→world). **Support: SUPPORTED**, with a calibration:
inquiry has *weak, peripheral* irreversibility (destructive measurement, finite budget,
belief-path-dependence), so the divergence is one of **centrality and degree**, not absolute
presence/absence — pervasive-and-constitutive in planning, special-case in inquiry.
**Consequence (the volume's headline):** this is the **exact seam** where the Knowing↔Acting
symmetry stops being an isomorphism — and it is *predicted by direction of fit* (mind→world
adds; world→mind unmakes), so the theory forecast its own break rather than hitting it
blindly. **Falsifier:** show planning's field is ergodic (no absorbing states — every failure
recoverable) or inquiry's is as non-ergodic as planning's (truth routinely made permanently
unknowable by inquiry moves).

## Objective 9 · Open problems
1. **Creative planning.** PT4 covers plans *within* a fixed action/space; where the plan
   requires *inventing* a new action or means (genuine creativity), the option set is not
   given, and PT4's completeness is UNKNOWN — the planning twin of Inquiry's *unconceived
   alternatives* frontier. OPEN.
2. **Open-world planning.** Acting under an incomplete model of the world (unknown
   preconditions/effects) — the plan is built over a frame that may omit the real
   dependencies; uncertifiable from inside (the dual of Inquiry Attack 2). OPEN.
3. **Infinite / unbounded horizon.** Plans toward ends with no terminal state (maintenance
   goals, ongoing missions) — where "completion" is undefined and the stopping rule has no
   natural terminus. OPEN.
4. **Multi-agent planning.** Plans whose success depends on other agents' concurrent plans
   (coordination, adversaries) — single-agent PT4 is silent; deferred to Vol VII Governance
   (as strategic decision was in Vol III). OPEN (scoped out).
5. **Planning without explicit goals.** Improvisation, exploration, open-ended play produce
   plan-like structure with no fixed goal (Objective 5's jazz case at the limit) — does PT5
   (elaboration of a commitment) survive when the commitment is to a *process/frame* rather
   than a *goal-state*? Partially (the frame is the commitment) — but a full account is
   OPEN.
6. **Continuous replanning.** When the world changes faster than plans can be completed,
   the plan/execution/revision loop never settles; the boundary between "a plan" and "a
   policy" blurs (a continuously-replanned plan approximates a policy). Where exactly Plan
   collapses into Policy under fast replanning is UNKNOWN. OPEN.

## Settled vs open (honest ledger)
- **Settled (SUPPORTED):** PT4 (the four-term primitive with Temporal induced); PT5 (plan =
  elaborated commitment; Planning extends Commitment as Inquiry extends Warrant); PT6
  (non-ergodicity — the located seam); the failure taxonomy's primitive-validation.
- **FALSIFIED (a genuine result, not a defeat):** PT2 (`Commitment + Temporal`); PT3 (the
  mission's `Commitment + Temporal + Dependency` — insufficient and mis-structured);
  "Temporal is a primitive of Plan."
- **Refined:** PT1 → PT1′ (No Dependency/Resource → No Temporal → No Plan).
- **Open / UNKNOWN:** creative (1), open-world (2), infinite-horizon (3), multi-agent (4),
  goal-less (5), continuous-replanning / Plan↔Policy collapse (6).

The pattern holds a sixth time — **determinate in the middle, open at the frontier** — but
Vol IV adds something the prior volumes did not: a **partial FALSIFICATION** that *locates a
structural divergence* (non-ergodicity, PT6) rather than merely extending the symmetry. The
mission's highest hope is met on the harder horn: the symmetry is not *just* pretty (it
broke where a real difference lives) **and** it is predictive (it forecast the break from
direction of fit). Both, which is the strongest available outcome — pursued in file 05.
