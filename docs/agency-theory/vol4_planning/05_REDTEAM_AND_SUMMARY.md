## Objective 8 · The assault on Planning = Inquiry

Attackers, as commissioned: reactive systems, reflexes, improvisation, emergent
coordination, swarm behaviour, habit, skill execution — plus the structural attacker the
recovery itself produced (irreversibility).

### Attack 1 — Reactive systems / reflexes: structured action with no planning
Reflexes and reactive controllers produce apt, goal-serving action with **no plan at all**
— so planning is not necessary for competent action, and identifying the action-stack with
"planning" over-reaches. **Verdict: SURVIVES (the attacker mislocates the claim).** The
symmetry is not "all action is planning" but "**planning** is to acting what **inquiry** is
to knowing" — the *deliberative, structured* stratum of each. Reactive action is the
*non-deliberative floor*, and it has an exact knowing-side twin: **perception** (immediate,
unstructured belief-fixation). `reflex : action :: perception : belief`. The attacker shows
both stacks have a reactive floor beneath the deliberative layer — which *confirms* the
symmetry's stratification rather than breaking it. SUPPORTED.

### Attack 2 — Improvisation / skill execution: fluent structure with no explicit plan
A jazz solo or an expert's fluid performance is structured and goal-directed yet not
pre-planned. **Verdict: SURVIVES (file 02's framed-reactive result).** The improviser
commits to a *frame* (a thin plan) and generates content reactively within it; the expert's
skill is *compiled* prior planning (habit, below). Neither is unplanned *structure from
nothing*. The knowing-side twin is **perceptual expertise / intuition** — fluent, unarticulated
belief-fixation that is not explicit inquiry. Both stacks have a *compiled/fluent* mode that
is neither the deliberative layer nor the raw reactive floor. Symmetric again. SUPPORTED.

### Attack 3 — Habit / skill: former plans that are no longer plans
A habit is action-structure that was *once* planned and is now automatic — so plan-structure
can exist with no live planning, which seems to sever plan from planning. **Verdict:
SURVIVES, sharpened.** A habit is a **compiled plan** — an elaborated commitment whose
deliberative scaffolding has been cached into a policy (Vol I). Its knowing-side twin is
**a memorized/entrenched belief** — once inquired, now simply held, its justification cached.
`habit : plan :: entrenched-belief : inquiry`. This is not a break; it is the *fourth* matched
stratum (deliberative / compiled / reactive / — ), and it maps cleanly on both sides.
SUPPORTED.

### Attack 4 — Emergent coordination / swarms: plan-like order with no planner
Ant colonies and flocks produce globally plan-like structure with **no plan-holder** — so
plan-structure needs no planning agent, refuting the elaboration theorem (PT5). **Verdict:
SURVIVES (Vol I's collective/teleonomic result applies).** Emergent order is **plan-*like*
without being a plan**: no agent *holds* the commitment; the structure is installed by local
rules + selection (teleonomy, Vol I). Its knowing-side twin is **emergent
information-structure without inquiry** — e.g. the "knowledge" implicit in a genome or a
market price, which no one *inquired*. Both stacks have an *emergent, unheld* analog of their
deliberative product. The elaboration theorem is about *held* plans and is untouched; the
swarm is correctly outside it, exactly as evolved "knowledge" is outside inquiry. SUPPORTED.

### Attack 5 — The structural attacker: irreversibility breaks the isomorphism (**the hit**)
The recovery's own finding (files 01, 03, 04 PT6): planning's field is **non-ergodic**
(consuming, irreversible, with absorbing states) while inquiry's is (idealizing) **ergodic**
(accumulative, revisable, truth ever-approachable). Actions have **delete-effects**;
observations do not. So the two activities are **not isomorphic** — planning carries an
irreducible primitive (Irreversibility/Resource) with no robust inquiry counterpart.
**Verdict: the STRONG claim `Planning ≅ Inquiry (isomorphism)` — FALSIFIED. The claim
`Planning and Inquiry share the same form (homomorphism)` — SURVIVES.** This is the
mission's prized outcome: a *real* break that *locates* where Knowing and Acting diverge,
and does so *predictably* (direction of fit: mind→world adds, world→mind unmakes). The
symmetry is a **form-preserving map whose kernel is exactly the irreversibility of action.**

### Attack 6 — Self-application: was this volume produced by planning or inquiry?
Writing Vol IV was **both**, and the two were distinguishable in the act: an **inquiry**
(recover the literature, reduce the Unknown about "what is a plan" — reversible, accumulative:
early wrong readings cost nothing, cross-checkable) *feeding* a **plan** (commit to the
ontology-first frame, elaborate six interdependent files in dependency order, under a finite
attention budget — with mild irreversibility: a committed framing shaped later files). The
inquiry half was ergodic; the writing half was mildly non-ergodic. **Verdict: SURVIVES, and
it instances PT6** — the very asymmetry the volume claims was present in producing it. A
volume that could not tell its own inquiry-phase from its own planning-phase would undercut
PT6; this one can. LIKELY.

### Attack 7 — "Homomorphism not isomorphism" is a retreat to save the thesis
Downgrading from ≅ to "same form, different field-physics" looks like moving the goalposts to
keep the symmetry alive. **Verdict: SURVIVES — because the downgrade is *forced by evidence*
and *predicted*, not chosen for convenience.** Three checks distinguish a principled downgrade
from a rescue: (i) it was *derived* (the irreversibility primitive fell out of the primitive
analysis and the failure taxonomy independently, files 01+03, before the symmetry was
re-examined); (ii) it *predicts* the located seam from the pre-existing direction-of-fit axis
(no new machinery); (iii) it is *falsifiable* (PT6's falsifier is stated). A goalpost-move
fails all three; this passes all three. **Residue:** the honest status is *homomorphism with
a named kernel* — stronger and more useful than either "perfect mirror" (false) or "mere
analogy" (empty), because it says *exactly how far* the mirror holds and *exactly where* it
stops. LIKELY→SUPPORTED.

## D10 · Summary — what Volume IV delivers

**The answer to "what is a plan?" (SUPPORTED):** a plan is a **commitment elaborated into a
dependency-structured, resource-bound, irreversible means toward a goal** — `Plan =
Commitment ⊕ Dependency ⊕ Irreversibility`, with **temporal order induced**, not primitive.
Planning is the activity of producing and revising plans; a plan is its revisable product,
distinct from goal, decision, policy, procedure, prediction, schedule, algorithm, and
execution.

**The load-bearing results — and the honest verdict on the mission's own hypothesis:**
1. **The mission's formula is FALSIFIED as stated** (`Commitment + Temporal + Dependency`):
   insufficient (omits Irreversibility, which owns the two most characteristic plan-deaths —
   resource collapse and corner-painting) and mis-structured (Temporal is the induced shadow
   of Dependency + Resource, and owns no failure of its own). This is a *result*, produced by
   recovery, tagged FALSIFIED — exactly the outcome the mission valued over a comfortable
   confirmation.
2. **`Planning ≅ Inquiry` is FALSIFIED as an isomorphism, SURVIVES as a homomorphism.** They
   share form — the Goal→Commitment→Plan spine mirrors Warrant→Inquiry (PT5: **Planning
   extends Commitment as Inquiry extends Warrant**), the closure/stopping rule is *the same*
   rule (PF9/PF10 = Inquiry's stopping misfirings), and the deliberative/compiled/reactive/
   emergent strata match 1:1 (red team). They **diverge** in field-physics: planning is
   **non-ergodic** (irreversible, consuming), inquiry is (idealizing) **ergodic**.
3. **The divergence *locates where Knowing and Acting part*** — the kernel of the
   Knowing↔Acting map is the **irreversibility of action**, which is *direction of fit made
   structural*: to know is to *add* (monotone, reversible); to act is to *unmake* (non-monotone,
   consuming). Part I could not contain this because it never touched the world.

**The meta-result the mission most wanted.** The symmetry is shown to be **neither mere
aesthetic nor perfect mirror.** It is a *predictive homomorphism with a named kernel*: it
predicted the structure of this volume in advance (the spine, the stopping rule, the strata —
all confirmed), *and* it predicted its own boundary (the irreversibility break, forecast from
direction of fit and then found by independent recovery). A theory that can predict both where
it holds and where it breaks — before writing the volume — has the stronger property the
mission asked for: explanatory power, not retrospective tidiness.

**How it sits in the stack.** Vol III handed Vol IV a Commitment; Vol IV elaborates it over
time and returns a **plan** — and, in doing so, hits the first *structural* limit of the
Knowing↔Acting mirror (non-ergodicity), which the remaining volumes must respect: **Execution
(Vol V)** is where irreversibility is *incurred* (the delete-effect actually fires);
**Learning (Vol VI)** is how the non-ergodic field's outcomes update the plan (the mirror of
inquiry's belief-update, now over a world that changed); **Governance (Vol VII)** is where
irreversibility becomes *ethical* (the veto over foreclosing, irreversible, multi-agent
action). The seam Vol IV located is the thread the rest of Part II runs along.

**What Volume IV does NOT do (scope):** single-agent (multi-agent → Vol VII), goal-given
(goal-less/creative planning → open), fixed-model (open-world → open); it does not prescribe
a planning *algorithm* (STRIPS/HTN/MDP remain the tame interior of PT4's space, unnamed as
axioms by design). It answers *what a plan is*, and — more valuably — it reports *exactly
where the symmetry that organized the whole stack stops being an identity*.

**One-line result:** `Plan = Commitment + Temporal + Dependency` — **FALSIFIED**; the truth is
`Plan = Commitment ⊕ Dependency ⊕ Irreversibility` (temporal induced); and **Planning ≅
Inquiry is false as an isomorphism but true as a form** — the mirror holds in structure and
breaks at irreversibility, which is precisely where *acting* stops being *knowing run
backward* and starts being *the spending of a world that does not come back*.
